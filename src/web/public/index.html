Background service

Keeps vos running after reboot. While enabled on AC power, caffeinate blocks idle sleep so the scheduler is not frozen (Energy Saver "sleep after 1 minute" is why a lid-open Mac still missed slots). Closing the lid on Apple Silicon still sleeps — use clamshell (power + display) or leave the lid open.
